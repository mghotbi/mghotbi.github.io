Research

Connecting processes.
Understanding recovery.

My research connects rhizosphere biology, soil carbon and nitrogen cycling, and host-associated microbiomes. I combine controlled experiments and process measurements with molecular data and quantitative analysis to understand how biological interactions respond to changing environments.

Soil–plant–microbe interactions

Plant genotype, microbial partners and environmental stress shape rhizosphere processes, nutrient cycling and plant performance. My work includes biological nitrification inhibition, microbial inoculation, flooding, herbivory and fungal pathogen stress.

Redox resilience

I investigate how hydrological disturbance reorganizes soil–plant–microbiome function, with a focus on electron-transfer processes and elemental coupling, and on the recovery process that follows flooding and drought.

Quantitative microbiomes

I integrate amplicon sequencing, metagenomics, metatranscriptomics and reproducible computation — including absolute-abundance analysis using spike-in controls — to connect microbial community patterns with biological function.

Developing independent research directions

Holobiont redox resilience across the maize domestication gradient, and HYDROREDOX: testing whether event-accessible electron-transfer architecture helps predict recovery after flood–drought cycles.

DspikeIn

Estimating Absolute Abundance from Microbial Spike-in Controls

A modular workflow for absolute microbial quantification using single spike-in taxa or synthetic microbial communities. Supports spike-in validation, preprocessing, scaling-factor estimation, absolute-abundance conversion, bias correction and normalization, with phyloseq and TreeSummarizedExperiment compatibility.

BiocManager::install("DspikeIn")

First time using Bioconductor?

Install BiocManager first with install.packages("BiocManager"), then run the command above.

R / CRAN · Version 1.0.6

HRRI

Diagnostics for Soil–Plant–Microbial Redox Recovery

Integrate longitudinal soil, plant and microbial observations to examine redox disturbance and recovery. Includes recovery signatures, fixed-reference domain scores, observation-coverage diagnostics, and conditional calculations of oxygen demand and accessible electron capacity.

install.packages("HRRI")

Fm

FunMel

From fungal confrontation photographs to matched-control results.

FunMel measures the colour, shape and image-darkness profiles of approximately round Zymoseptoria tritici colonies around another fungus. It separates target colonies from central fungal tissue and connected hyphal growth, preserves image provenance and custom metadata, and compares treatments with their own set controls.

Teaching and mentoring

From a biological question to a defensible conclusion.

I teach microbial ecology, bioinformatics and statistics by connecting mechanisms, experiments, measurements and interpretation. Reproducible code is part of the scientific argument.

My mentoring spans undergraduate, graduate and early-career researchers at UIUC, MTSU and Kiel. I emphasize clear expectations, constructive feedback and growing independence, with multiple entry points for students from different disciplinary backgrounds.
