Research-use protocol. This SOP combines established methods with maize- and wheat-oriented implementation guidance. 02 / Sampling & experimental design

Rules that apply to every assay

  • Replication. At least five biological replicates per genotype × treatment × time point; average technical duplicates before statistics.
  • Time points. Baseline, peak stress, early and late recovery, with time-matched controls at every point.
  • Time of day. Harvest within the same 1–2 h window and interleave treatments.
  • Two sample streams. Fresh tissue for staining and live assays; tissue frozen in liquid N₂ within 10 s for extract-based assays.
  • Flooded plants. Re-exposure of hypoxic tissue to air triggers a reoxygenation ROS burst, so the interval from de-submergence to freezing is fixed and recorded.

03 / Quality control

Defensible numbers

CriterionStarting acceptance
Standard curveR² ≥ 0.99; all samples inside the range
Technical CV< 10%
Spike recovery (P4)80–120%, checked per treatment matrix
Pooled QC driftWithin ± 15% of the running mean

Resilience is summarised as impact (log response ratio at peak stress) and recovery relative to time-matched controls, following Ingrisch & Bahn 2018 ↗.
