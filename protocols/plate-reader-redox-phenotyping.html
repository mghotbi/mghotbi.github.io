02 / Before you begin

Match the assay to the reader

  • Fluorescence: peroxide assays using Amplex Red require appropriate excitation/emission settings and black plates.
  • UV absorbance: catalase at 240 nm and APX at 290 nm require compatible reader optics and UV-transparent plates.
  • Visible absorbance: check the specified wavelengths, plate chemistry and blank corrections for each assay.

The full SOP gives assay-specific settings. Read Part A before preparing samples or allocating plates.

Interpret measurements within their scope. H₂O₂ assays measure peroxide under the stated assay conditions; antioxidant activity and oxidative-damage measurements are complementary endpoints, not a single “total ROS” measurement. This research-use SOP includes pilot conditions and proposed QC criteria that require validation for the local tissue, instrument and treatment matrix.
